SED interactive presentation (v4): "A Framework for Future-Ready Education"
Open index.html in a modern browser. No server needed (internet is only used for the Google fonts).

Files
  index.html        page structure, title and footer text
  css/styles.css    all styling and animation
  js/content.js     EDIT THIS to change the text in the hover boxes (BRIEFS)
  js/app.js         stage content, navigation and behaviour
  assets/logo.png   SED logo

13 stages, each with two parts: part 1 = text, part 2 = visuals (the small dots on the line).
 1 Curiosity   2 Learning crisis   3 A new layer   4 Why diagnose   5 Beyond access   6 Meet SED
 7 How SED works   8 What is different   9 Ecosystem   10 Teachers   11 Parents   12 Our vision   13 The future
The last page animates the punch lines one after another (timing: PUNCH_GAP in js/app.js).

Keys: Left/Right = previous/next part, Home/End = first/last, R = replay, S = sources, F = fullscreen, Esc = close.
